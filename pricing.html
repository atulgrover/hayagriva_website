Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on the three-tier model, fortnightly sync, and credit accounting.

Is the Hayagriva Desktop IDE really 100% free?
Yes. You can download the IDE, parse your own PDF/Word/Excel case records, configure local LLMs (like Ollama or Mistral), and run sovereign RAG searches without ever paying a fee. Your confidential case files never leave your machine.
Why do Knowledge Vaults require a fortnightly subscription?
Commercial law, IBC jurisprudence, and NCLT/NCLAT rulings evolve every two weeks. Our legal curation team continuously analyzes and indexes newly gazetted rulings into Haya Jurisprudence Graph™ knowledge graphs. The subscription covers continuous curation and delivers signed delta packs directly to your IDE every 14 days.
What is "Agent Settling" and how does the prompt tuner work?
Every legal chamber has a distinct drafting style. When an agent produces an output that doesn't meet your firm's standards, you click "Refine Agent". The local prompt tuner analyzes your critique, generates prompt diffs, updates few-shot examples, and hot-reloads the agent until it is fully calibrated ("settled") to your preferences.
Do Resolution Bazaar (RBZ) credits expire?
No. Diligence credits in your ₹25,000 prepaid pack remain in your account until consumed. You can use them across multiple CIRP assignments throughout the calendar year with zero expiration pressure.
How are Section 29A reports authenticated for NCLT?
Each Resolution Bazaar screening report includes a cryptographic SHA-256 hash, timestamped registry cross-match evidence, and director network trees, creating a court-admissible audit certificate ready for submission to the Committee of Creditors (CoC).
Can I top up credits whenever my wallet balance runs low?
Yes. You can instantly top up via Razorpay inside Resolution Bazaar or directly from the IDE's Resolution Bazaar Gateway settings panel. New credits are credited instantly to your chamber token.
