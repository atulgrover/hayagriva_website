## 1. Executive Summary & The "AI Drip Resolution" Philosophy

Under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC, 2016), Corporate Insolvency Resolution Processes (CIRP) operate under unforgiving statutory timelines (14 days for claims collation, 30 days for CoC constitution, 105 days for Information Memorandum, and a hard 180/330-day ceiling). 

Traditionally, Resolution Professionals (RPs) and legal counsels suffer from **The Batch Backlog Trap**:
- Large packets of creditor claims, financial audits, and contracts arrive continuously but sit unread in inboxes until statutory deadlines create panic.
- Rushed reviews lead to severe personal regulatory liability: IBBI show-cause notices, NCLT cost impositions, and litigation delays caused by overlooked Section 29A disqualifications or avoidance transactions (Sections 43, 45, 66).

### The Solution: "AI Drip Resolution"
**Continuous Ingestion -> Instant Background Analysis -> Zero Missed Deadlines -> Ironclad Liability Shield.**

Instead of batching work when deadlines approach, local autonomous agents get to work **the millisecond any information is received**. A bank statement, a Form C claim, or an email is immediately ingested, parsed against the IBC statutory checklist, cross-referenced with financial ledgers, and flagged for discrepancies in real time.

The entire site messaging is built upon two complementary pillars:

   - Instant Claims Collation (Form B, C, D, E)
   - NCLT Court-Formatted Drafts & Pleadings
   - Auto-Generated Information Memorandum (IM)
   - 100% Offline Case Evidence Binders
2. **Liability & Risk Shield**:
   - Zero Missed Statutory Deadlines (14, 30, 105, 180, 330 days)
   - Automatic Section 29A Debarment & Disqualification Screening
   - Section 43/45/66 Avoidance Transaction Early Warning Flags

## 5. Verification & Acceptance Criteria
1. **Zero Unexplained Developer Terms:** No raw references to Monaco, AST, ONNX, GGUF, FTS5, LSP, or token counts in user-facing copy.
2. **Professional Legal Resonance:** An Insolvency Professional or Advocate reading any page immediately understands the value, workflows, and risk protection.
3. **Synchronized Navigation:** All changes in terminology remain consistent with the top header navigation and footer links.
4. **Visual Excellence:** Retain the clean, premium styling, interactive cards, and responsive layouts.
