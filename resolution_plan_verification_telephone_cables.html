⚠️ Statutory Requisition Schedule (Pre-CoC Voting Addendum)

The Resolution Professional must issue this formal requisition to the Prospective Resolution Applicant to cure the following items prior to conducting electronic voting under Section 30(4):

🚨 Item 1: Invalidation of Avoidance Proceedings Abatement (Clause 3.4.17)
Defect: The plan states that non-final avoidance applications under §43/§45/§50/§66 "shall abate" and RA has sole discretion to pursue or drop them.
Mandatory Remedy: RA must submit an addendum deleting the abatement clause. The plan must confirm that all avoidance proceedings filed by the RP survive NCLT approval and shall be prosecuted by the RP / Monitoring Committee for the exclusive benefit of Financial Creditors.
⚡ Item 2: Operational Creditor Liquidation Value Certification (§30(2)(b))
Defect: Trade OCs receive ₹20.00 Lakhs against ₹18.16 Crores admitted claims (1.10%).
Mandatory Remedy: The RP must formally certify in Form H that based on the registered valuation reports, the liquidation value attributable to Operational Creditors under Section 53 is nil or does not exceed ₹20.00 Lakhs.
🛡️ Item 3: Physical Deposit of Performance Bank Guarantee (Reg. 36B(4A))
Defect: Undertaking to furnish ₹15.11 Crores PBG is recorded in the plan.
Mandatory Remedy: The physical Bank Guarantee of ₹15.11 Crores (10% of outlay) must be deposited with the Lead Financial Creditor and confirmed in Form H before filing with NCLT.
