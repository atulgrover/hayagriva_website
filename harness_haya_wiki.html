Architecture

Three-Layer Knowledge Architecture

Inspired by Andrej Karpathy's LLM-Wiki pattern — raw sources remain immutable; the wiki is entirely LLM-maintained.

Layer 1
Raw Sources

PDFs, DOCX, XLSX and standalone markdown files in the case folder. Immutable — the IMS pipeline reads from them but never modifies them. This is the ground truth of the case.

raw/ • documents/ • You write, IMS reads
Layer 2
The Wiki

LLM-generated and maintained markdown pages. Sources summaries, entity pages, concept pages, synthesised Q&A insights, and the machine-written INDEX.md catalog. The LLM writes it; you read it.

wiki/ • sources/ • insights/ • LLM writes, you read
Layer 3
Schema & Config

case_manifest.json and AGENTS.md conventions that tell the LLM how to structure wiki pages, what ingest mode to use, which statutory milestones to track, and how the entity graph is scoped.

case_manifest.json • Co-evolved by you & LLM

Operations

Three Core Wiki Operations

IngestAuto-Brief

Source Ingest → Wiki Page

After a document is indexed, the LLM generates a wiki/sources/<stem>.md summary page (300–500 words) covering key entities, document type, financial figures, and any warning flags. This is immediately added to INDEX.md.

Auto-creates source page Updates INDEX.md
QueryAnswer Filing

RAG Answer → Wiki Insight

Every agent chat response includes a "Save to Case Wiki" button. When an answer cites ≥ 4 distinct documents and exceeds 300 words, a subtle chip auto-suggests filing. Filed insights carry frontmatter: source_query, date, documents_used, agent.

One-click filing FTS5-indexed instantly
LintKiller Differentiator

Case Wiki Lint Audit

A periodic LLM health-check pass across all wiki pages and case_kv_dictionary.json. Outputs structured cards in the Case Action Inbox. No other legal PKM tool has this capability — the wiki actively defends itself against stale and contradictory knowledge.

Contradiction flags Inbox cards

Lint Operation — 8 Audit Checks

Contradictions
Mismatched amounts between claims_registry.md and avoidance_ledger.md entries across different filings.
Stale Claims
case_facts.md fields that are blank [UNKNOWN] but may be derivable from already-ingested documents via RAG.
Orphan Wiki Pages
Wiki pages with no inbound references from other pages or from the INDEX.md catalog — unlinked knowledge islands.
Missing Concept Pages
Important concepts mentioned in source summaries but lacking their own dedicated wiki page.
Orphan Chunks
Document vectors with zero retrieval count — knowledge that has never been surfaced in any RAG query and may be improperly tagged.
Statutory Milestone Gaps
CIRP T0→T330 milestones in timeline.md that lack a corresponding ingested filing (e.g., Day 30 CoC notice has no meeting notice document).
Missing Cross-References
References from one wiki page pointing to another that has no corresponding page — broken internal links in the knowledge graph.
Suggested Investigations
Data gaps that could be filled with additional document ingestion — new sources suggested by the LLM based on patterns in existing wiki pages.
