Why Domain-SLMs Surpass Frontier LLMs for Regulated Work

Large General-Purpose LLMs (70B–405B) excel at broad conversational banter, but introduce unacceptable failure modes in mission-critical legal, financial, and institutional workflows.

Complete Air-Gapped Sovereignty

Law firms, insolvency professionals, banks, and healthcare providers operate under statutory confidentiality duties (e.g. Attorney-Client Privilege, IBBI Regulations, HIPAA, GDPR). Domain SLMs run completely on local device memory via GGUF / llamafile with zero telemetry.

Deep Statutory Determinism

Domain SLMs are pre-trained and fine-tuned on actual judicial rulings, statutory codes, balance sheet formats, and regulatory circulars. Their attention mechanisms are anchored to rigorous statutory rules rather than generalized internet prose.

Commodity Hardware Economics

Running 4-bit quantized (Q4_K_M) 2.9B or 7B SLMs requires only 2GB to 5GB of unified memory. Practitioners can execute instant local inference directly on standard MacBooks, laptops, or secure air-gapped desktops with sub-second token generation.

Our HuggingFace → GGUF Foundry Pipeline

How any professional domain SLM is packaged into offline execution weights inside foundry/.

Foundry Automated
Step 01 • Ingest
HF Safetensors
Download domain model weights, tokenizer configs, and vocabularies from Hugging Face Hub.
Step 02 • Convert
Remap to GGUF
Execute convert_hf_to_gguf.py to produce uncompressed FP16 GGUF tensor format.
Step 03 • Patch Vocab
BPE Byte Sanitizer
Run patch_gguf_vocab.py to correct Indian language or domain byte tokens (<0xNN>).
Step 04 • Quantize
Q4_K_M Packaging
Apply medium k-quantization to compress the model to ~1.7GB for ultra-fast local CPU/GPU execution.
