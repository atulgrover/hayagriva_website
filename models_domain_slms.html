Why Domain-SLMs Surpass Frontier LLMs for Regulated Work

Large General-Purpose LLMs (70B–405B) excel at broad conversational banter, but introduce unacceptable failure modes in mission-critical legal, financial, and institutional workflows.

Complete Air-Gapped Sovereignty

Law firms, insolvency professionals, banks, and healthcare providers operate under statutory confidentiality duties (e.g. Attorney-Client Privilege, IBBI Regulations, HIPAA, GDPR). Domain SLMs run completely on local device memory via an isolated hardware enclave with zero external telemetry.

Deep Statutory Determinism

Domain SLMs are specialized and aligned on judicial rulings, statutory codes, balance sheet formats, and regulatory circulars. Their attention mechanisms are anchored to rigorous statutory rules rather than generalized internet prose.

Commodity Hardware Economics

Running hardware-quantized domain engines requires minimal unified memory. Practitioners can execute instant local inference directly on standard MacBooks, laptops, or secure air-gapped desktops with sub-second token generation.
